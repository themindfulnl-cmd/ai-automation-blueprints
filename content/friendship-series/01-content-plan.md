# Friendship series · content plan

**The Mindful NL** · for parents · based on *Alles over vrienden* (Felicity Brooks, ill. Mar Ferrero, Usborne; NL translation Els Peeters)

- **Run:** Mon 30 Nov 2026 → Mon 18 Jan 2027, Mon–Fri. No posts on 25 Dec or 1 Jan.
- **Mix:** 15 reels · 16 carousels · 3 stories (post 23 also gets a short reel version)
- **Every post:** what the skill is → why it matters → one thing to do at home → praise line → soft class invite
- **Every caption ends with:** "Inspired by Alles over vrienden (Usborne)." / "Geïnspireerd op Alles over vrienden (Usborne)."
- **Status key:** ✅ sample ready for your review · ⏳ written after you approve the style
