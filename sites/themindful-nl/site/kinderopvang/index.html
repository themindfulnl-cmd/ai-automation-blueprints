For daycare & preschool groups · ages 4–6

Kids yoga & mindfulness for daycare

Playful movement, animal yoga and simple breathing games for children aged 4–6. Rainbow Feelings adapts the method for this age — short, sensory, and always in motion.

Request a quoteSee the approach
Sound familiar?

Overstimulation starts early

In a group of young children, joy, sadness and frustration switch rapidly. A fixed, recurring calm moment provides structure — not by making children sit still, but by moving, breathing, and imitating an animal together.

The approach

Movement, animals, and a calm moment

For children aged 4–6, the session is mostly about the body, not long explanations. Three simple building blocks form a recognisable ritual.

Animal yoga

Frog, elephant, butterfly — recognisable movements children love to imitate, giving energy somewhere to go.

Breathing games

Simple techniques like 'smell the flower, blow out the candle' — easy to remember, even without many words.

A sensory calm moment

Soft music, slow breathing and a fixed spot — so the transition to calm becomes predictable.

Pedagogical staff actively join in, and can keep using the movements and breathing games afterwards during the daily routine.

Frequently asked questions

Is this suitable for preschoolers who don't want to talk much yet?

Yes. The session is mostly physical and sensory — imitating animals, moving, simple breathing. Language supports but doesn't lead, so it works well for children still developing their language skills.

Do you also work with children younger than 4?

No, this programme is developed for children aged 4 to 6. The movement, stories and group structure are designed specifically for this developmental stage.

What does a session or programme cost for our location?

That depends on group size, frequency, and the format you choose (one-off, 8-week block, or ongoing). We work with a tailored quote — get in touch and you'll receive a proposal within a few days.

How many children can join a session?

Usually up to about 12 children per session, appropriate for the age group and keeping enough individual attention.

Do you need a separate room?

No. The group room or a quiet corner of the play area is enough. We agree on this together beforehand.

Is this a replacement for existing activities, or an addition?

An addition. Most locations schedule this as a fixed recurring moment alongside their existing activity offering.

How do you handle child safety?

We take this seriously and are happy to discuss it during the introductory conversation, so it fits within your protocols.
