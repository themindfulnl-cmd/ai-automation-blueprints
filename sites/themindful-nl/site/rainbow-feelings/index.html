The pattern we follow

The 3 friends inside every story

Whatever story we tell — a flower, a lion, a dinosaur — these three friends always show up. They give your child a vocabulary and a real tool for big feelings.

G

General Grumpy

When something feels hard

We welcome him. We breathe. He's not the enemy — he's the messenger that we need a little help.

C

Captain Calm

When we breathe

We do our best. We work steadily — one pose at a time.

H

Hip Hip Hooray

When we did the work

We celebrate with our whole body. Joy that's earned.

★ The Magic Sequence

The three lines every child takes home at the end of class

General Grumpy visits → we welcome him, we breathe.
Captain Calm comes → we try our best.

The Story Yoga Arc

Every story — The Seed, Leo the Lion Cub, Rex the Dinosaur — follows the same 6 phases. Children feel the structure, recognise each moment, and know what comes next.

1🥚

Safe start

Child's Pose. The story opens softly.

2⚡

The challenge

Something feels hard. A leaf, a sharing moment, a waiting.

3🟠

General Grumpy arrives

We feel the feeling in the body. Fists, frown, then let it go.

4🌬️

Smell the Flower, Blow the Candle

Our signature breath. × 3 rounds. Slow and steady.

5🟢

Captain Calm — working poses

Poses that do the work: Tree, Warrior, Cat-Cow, Butterfly, Boat.

6🎉

HIP HIP HOORAY

Jumps, proud poses, a collective cheer. The work is done.

Common questions

Mindfulness for Kids — Frequently Asked Questions

Everything parents, teachers, and schools ask about The Mindful Kids method.

What is The Mindful Kids method?

+

The Mindful Kids method combines kids yoga, breathing, stories, and emotional vocabulary for children aged 5–12. Children meet three signature friends — General Grumpy, Captain Calm, and Hip Hip Hooray — and practise a simple sequence: notice, breathe, try, celebrate. Confirmed classes appear on the Classes page; school and group visits are available by request.

What age is The Mindful Kids for?

+

Our weekly classes and holiday camps are designed for children aged 5–12 years. The method is age-appropriate at every step — younger children focus on play, movement, and story; older children explore breath, self-regulation, and reflection. Siblings inside this range are welcome to join the same class.

How much does it cost?

+

Weekly group classes cost €15 per class. The first class is a free trial, so your child can meet the group before you decide.

Where are the classes held?

+

The Mindful Kids is based in Hoofddorp. The Classes page lists confirmed weekly groups. School, BSO, childcare, camp, and private programmes can also be arranged in Amstelveen, Amsterdam, Haarlem, Nieuw-Vennep, and surrounding areas.

How long is each class and what happens in it?

+

Each class is 45 minutes: Welcome circle (5 min), balance games with yoga ball and bricks (10 min), sun salutations (5 min), meet the 3 friends and Story Yoga (15 min), and rain dance plus body scan (10 min). Every class follows the same predictable flow so children feel safe to settle in quickly.

What is the Rainbow Feelings method and the 3 Friends?

+

Rainbow Feelings is the emotional-literacy foundation of The Mindful Kids method. Children learn to recognise and name big feelings through three signature friends: General Grumpy (the big uncomfortable feelings), Captain Calm (the breath and the pause), and Hip Hip Hooray (the celebration after we get through). It teaches the magic sequence — notice, breathe, try, celebrate.

How does mindfulness help kids build confidence?

+

Classes give children chances to try a pose, pause, breathe, and speak about feelings in simple language. The aim is practice: children build familiarity with tools they can choose to use at home or school.

Do you offer a free trial class?

+

Yes — every child's first class is completely free. Book a free trial class at https://www.themindful.nl/lessen. You and your child can try a full session, meet the teacher, and decide afterwards whether to join the 8-week block.
