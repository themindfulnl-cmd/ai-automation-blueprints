Our method

Three friends who live in every child's heart

Big feelings, given a name. Once children meet them, the feelings stop being scary — they become friends a child can welcome. These three characters are the heart of Rainbow Feelings.

G

General Grumpy

"It's too hard. I'm stuck."

Shows up when something feels hard, frustrating, or unfair. We welcome him, tap the heart, take 3 breaths.

C

Captain Calm

"I am calm. I can try."

Shows up when we breathe. Our signature breath: Smell the Flower, Blow the Candle — once a child tries it, they never forget it.

H

Hip Hip Hooray

"I did it! I am proud."

Shows up when we did the hard work. Celebrate with the whole body — Mountain, Warrior, Star. Joy that's earned.

Our signature tool

Smell the Flower · Blow the Candle

One concrete breathing tool every child takes home. Simple, body-led, and impossible to forget — works on the playground, at the dinner table, or anywhere a feeling suddenly gets big.

One skill, three breaths — tuned by age

Groep 3–6 · playful

Smell the Flower, Blow the Candle

A long, slow inhale through the nose, a soft exhale through the mouth. Simple, body-led, and they use it everywhere.

All ages

Box Breathing

In 4, hold 4, out 4, hold 4 — traced on your hand. The exact breath elite athletes, pilots and astronauts use before the big moment.

Groep 7–8 · more grown-up

Musical Breathing

Breathing on the beat — inhale on the build, exhale on the drop. Feels like music, not a toddler exercise, so older kids actually want to do it.

What happens in one session

The 45-minute session, phase by phase

A movement-led arc — bodies stay active throughout, hearts and minds settle by the end.

0:002 min

1. Biggest, brightest smile

A full-body wake-up. Kids drop the morning, find the room, find each other.

0:028 min

2. Sun salutations

A kid-friendly Surya Namaskar — taught playfully so it feels like a game, not a workout.

0:102 min

3. Meet the 3 friends

Quick story-time. Children meet General Grumpy, Captain Calm, Hip Hip Hooray.

0:1216 min

4. Story yoga · the heart of the class

A full story arc told through poses — children feel resilience in their bodies, not just their ears.

0:284 min

5. Pair breathing

Knee-to-knee with a partner — practising the signature breath together. Connection + memory.

0:3213 min

6. Rain dance & body scan

Whole-body stretch flow → legs up → rest pose → guided body scan → calm close.

What sticks

What every child leaves with

A class without a take-home is just an hour. A class with one becomes a tool they use for years.

01

A breathing tool they'll actually use.

Smell the Flower, Blow the Candle. Simple, body-led, and impossible to forget. Works in the playground, at the dinner table, at the dentist.

02

Three new friends in their head.

When the next hard moment comes, they don't panic — they recognise it. "That's General Grumpy. He's here. Now I breathe."

03

When the whole class knows the three friends, kids start naming feelings out loud — to each other, to teachers, at home. Hidden becomes seen.

Sample story · theme: not giving up

The Boy Who Cleaned the Ocean

This really happened. Boyan Slat, sixteen, from Delft, went diving in Greece — and saw plastic everywhere. He wanted to help. But it felt like too much.

Machines broke. The news said he was failing. Each time he named the feeling, breathed, and tried again. In 2019 the machine worked — millions of kilos of plastic removed, across thirteen countries.

The lesson: hard feelings come back — that's normal, that's the work. Name them, breathe, try a little. That's how you become who you become.

Told through 10 poses

From Child's Pose (small, stuck) to Tree with wide arms (Hip Hip Hooray) — children feel the story in their body, not just their ears.
