Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you want to know about kids yoga, mindfulness, and the Rainbow Feelings method.

🧘 About Our Classes

What is kids yoga and mindfulness?+

Kids yoga combines gentle yoga poses, breathing exercises, and mindfulness activities designed specifically for children aged 5-12. Our Rainbow Feelings method uses 7 colours, 7 emotions, and 7 characters to help children understand and manage their feelings through play, movement, and storytelling.

Is this just a yoga class?+

No — yoga is only the vehicle. Our classes are a complete emotional development program: children build self-confidence, learn to recognise and regulate big emotions, train focus and resilience, and discover calm they can find again at home or at school. Parents typically come to us for the confidence and emotional skills — the yoga is the playful way we get there.

My child is struggling — with big emotions, tension, or low self-confidence. Can this help?+

This is exactly who we are here for. Children who experience big emotions, meltdowns, worry, fear of failure, high sensitivity, or low self-confidence learn practical tools in our classes: breathing techniques, naming feelings, and finding calm in their own body. Our philosophy is 'Prevention, Not Therapy' — we build skills early so small struggles don't grow into big ones. The first trial class is free, so you can simply see if it fits your child. (For serious or persistent concerns, we always advise also consulting your GP or school doctor — our program complements professional care, it does not replace it.)

What age group are your classes for?+

Our classes are designed for children aged 5 to 12 years old. This is the critical window when children's brains are most receptive to learning emotional regulation, self-awareness, and coping skills that will serve them for life.

Does my child need previous yoga experience?+

Not at all! Our classes are designed for complete beginners. We make everything playful and accessible. Children learn through games, stories, and creative activities — not through traditional yoga instruction.

What should my child wear/bring to class?+

Comfortable clothing that allows movement (leggings, t-shirt, etc.). No shoes needed — we practice barefoot or in socks. We provide all yoga mats and materials. Just bring a water bottle!

🌈 Rainbow Feelings Method

What is the Rainbow Feelings method?+

Rainbow Feelings is our unique method that connects 7 colours to 7 emotions through 7 lovable characters. Each colour represents a feeling (Red = Love, Orange = Courage, Yellow = Joy, Green = Peace, Blue = Calm, Indigo = Wisdom, Violet = Imagination). Children learn to identify, name, and manage their emotions through yoga, breathwork, stories, and creative activities.

Is there scientific evidence that mindfulness helps children?+

Yes! Research published in Frontiers in Human Neuroscience shows that breathing exercises measurably increase vagal tone in children. A 2019 study in the Journal of Clinical Psychology found that children who practised breathing exercises for just 5 minutes daily showed significantly lower cortisol levels after four weeks. Children aged 5-12 are in a "sensitive period" when their brains are especially receptive to learning emotional regulation skills.

How is this different from regular kids yoga?+

While regular kids yoga focuses mainly on poses and physical movement, our Rainbow Feelings approach integrates emotional intelligence, breathwork, journaling, and character-based storytelling. We don't just teach kids to touch their toes — we teach them to understand their hearts. Our tagline says it all: Strong Hearts, Calm Minds.

📍 Locations & Booking

Where do you offer classes?+

We currently offer classes in Amstelveen, Amsterdam, Hoofddorp, Haarlem, and Nieuw-Vennep. All locations are in the Noord-Holland and Zuid-Holland provinces of the Netherlands. Visit our location pages for specific schedules and addresses.

Can I try a class before committing?+

Yes! We offer free trial classes so your child can experience the Rainbow Feelings method before you commit. The free Mindful Monday taster in Amstelveen took place on 5 October. The seven-class series begins on 19 October; see our Mindful Monday page for details.

Do you offer classes at schools?+

Yes! We offer tailored mindfulness and yoga programs for primary schools. Our school programs include workshops, multi-week courses, and teacher training. Contact us at themindfulnl@gmail.com to discuss options for your school.

How much do classes cost?+

Our pricing varies by program. Trial classes are currently free (€0) in Amstelveen. Regular class packages and private sessions have different pricing — check our Classes page for current rates. We believe every child deserves access to emotional tools.

👨‍👩‍👧 For Parents

Can parents stay during the class?+

For regular kids classes, we encourage parents to drop off and pick up. This gives children independence and helps them focus. For our Parent-Child Bonding classes, parents actively participate alongside their child — it's a beautiful shared experience!

My child is highly sensitive / gifted — is this suitable?+

Absolutely! Our Rainbow Feelings method is especially beneficial for highly sensitive and gifted children. These children often experience emotions more intensely and need safe ways to process them. Our methods teach self-regulation, emotional vocabulary, and coping tools that are particularly valuable for sensitive children.

What results can I expect?+

Parents typically notice improvements within 4-6 weeks: better emotional regulation, improved focus, calmer bedtime routines, and children who can name their feelings instead of acting them out. Many parents tell us their children use the breathing exercises independently at home and school.
