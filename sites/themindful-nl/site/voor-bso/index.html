Our technique

Three superfriends live in every child.

No abstract theory — three characters children recognise immediately, and that already have a name on the floor after one session.

General Grumpy

the friend who warns you

He shows up when something feels hard. Not naughty — he's just giving you a signal.

Captain Calm

the friend who helps you breathe

He shows you how to settle your head, with one simple breath.

Yay Hooray

the friend who celebrates you

He makes the proud feeling stick around — even when nobody's watching.

Musical breathing

Breathing that feels like music.

In with the music, hold, out with the music. It feels like a song, not an exercise — so even the toughest ten-year-olds join in laughing. It's the same rhythmic breathing top athletes and pilots use right before their big moment.

Signature Nº3

The Car Wash

A body scan, disguised as a game.

Water on your head, your eyes, your shoulders, your whole body. Then we lie down and let ourselves dry — the drying IS the body scan, from the feet all the way to the head. Five minutes of lying still: for many children the only genuinely quiet moment of their day.

Frequently asked questions

What does Superfriends cost for our BSO?

We work with a tailored quote. The price depends on the number of locations, how many age groups you want, and whether it's a 6-week block or a holiday programme. Request a quote and you'll receive a proposal within a few days.

What's the difference between Superfriends and Heart Detective?

Superfriends is the ongoing programme: weekly sessions during BSO hours, booked as a 6-week block per age group. Heart Detective is the holiday programme: a single 2-hour session during school holidays, in which children work through a complete detective story. Many BSOs combine the two.

How long is a session?

A Superfriends session runs 45 minutes, all materials included. The Heart Detective holiday programme runs 2 hours.

Can we start with just one location?

Yes, and we'd recommend it. Most organisations start with one location and one age group. If it works, we scale up together to more groups or more locations.

Can all three age groups run on the same afternoon?

Yes. Three groups back to back at one location, on one afternoon, works well — for many BSOs that's the easiest way to plan it.

Do you need a separate room?

No. An activity room, gym hall, or a quiet part of the outdoor play area is enough. We agree on this together beforehand.

How many children can join?

Usually up to about 15 children per session, so there's still enough personal attention. For larger groups we're happy to discuss how to organise it — by splitting, for example.

Do our pedagogical staff need to prepare anything?

No. I bring all the materials and lead the session myself. The only thing that stays behind is the language: after a few weeks your team starts asking “where is Captain Calm right now?” — and that keeps working the rest of the week.

Does this work for mixed-age groups?

Yes. We have three age variants (4–6, 7–9 and 10–12). For a mixed group we discuss beforehand which variant or combination fits best.

How do you handle child safety?

We take this seriously and are happy to discuss it during the introductory conversation, so it fits within your own protocols.
