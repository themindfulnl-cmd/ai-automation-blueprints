Recommend us to your child's school

Would you like these classes at your child's school or after-school club? Tell us. We'll contact the school and try to arrange a meeting about hosting classes there.

How it works

  1. 1

    You tell us which school it is

    The name is enough. Know the office email address? Even better.

  2. 2

    We get in touch

    We approach the school and try to arrange a meeting to explain the classes or give a trial lesson.

  3. 3

    You can forward it too

    You get a ready-written message. A school reads it very differently coming from one of its own parents — so it helps enormously.

The school's name stays private — it is never published on the website. And we only mention your name to the school if you give permission.
