one day, one village, one breath

This is not five separate yoga classes

Each week has one theme running through all the classes. While grandma learns “first breathe, then react” at chair yoga at 15:00, her grandchild learns exactly the same thing at 16:00 — through a story with three little friends. At the dinner table, they talk about it together.

That's the idea. 🌿

Mama & Baby — A Soft Start

When was the last time something was for you?

Gentle yoga for mama — back, shoulders, hips — while your little one lies on the mat beside you. We end with the sweetest moment: breathing with your baby on your chest. Babies borrow calm from a calm body. Crying, feeding, changing? All part of it.

Parent & Toddler — Stomp, Breathe, Cheer!

Making toddlers sit still? We don't even try.

A yoga adventure together: sneaking like cats, jumping like frogs. Along the way we stomp like General Grumpy, smell a flower like Captain Calm and cheer like Hip Hip Hooray. Without a single word of explanation, your toddler learns what to do with a grumpy moment.

Chair Yoga 55+ — Breath & Balance

“Am I too stiff for this?” No.

Everything happens on or next to a chair — no mat, no floor, no changing clothes. Looser shoulders and back, gentle balance practice, and a way of breathing that quiets the head. Tea afterwards, of course.

Kids Yoga — The 3 Friends

Big feelings in a small body — every parent knows it.

Every week a story-yoga adventure with three friends who live in every head: General Grumpy, Captain Calm and Hip Hip Hooray. No lecture — one trick your child truly takes home: first breathe, then react. Pure gold at home.

Breath & Awareness — Land Your Day

Monday, 5 PM. Your head is still full of the day.

Gentle movement to get out of the head, breathing (longer out than in), then the real work: noticing thoughts without being dragged along. No incense, no vagueness. At 17:45 you walk out — landed, right on time for dinner.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Mindful Monday cost?

The full seven-class series is €100. Seven single classes would cost €105, so the series saves you €5. If the full series isn't possible, you can book and pay for a single class in advance for €15.

How do I register?

Choose the class that suits you below and fill in the form. You'll receive an email to confirm your registration. Your place is confirmed once payment is received.

What if a class is cancelled or I cannot attend?

A class runs with at least three paid registrations. If we cancel a class, we'll refund that class. If you can't attend, cancel at least 24 hours in advance for a refund of that class.

Do I need yoga experience?

No. No experience needed, no sports outfit needed. All classes are beginner-friendly and chair yoga happens entirely on or next to a chair.

When does Mindful Monday start?

The free taster day on 5 October has passed. There is no class on 12 October. The series runs on 19/10, 26/10, 2/11, 9/11, 16/11, 23/11 and 30/11.

Where does Mindful Monday take place?

Huis Elsrijk, Graaf Aelbrechtlaan 138, 1181 SZ Amstelveen. Every Monday, there are five classes in one afternoon: from mama & baby (13:00) to breath & awareness for adults (17:00).

Is parking easy, and can I come by bus?

Yes. It's a blue zone: with a parking disc you park free for 2 hours, exactly enough for your class. Coming by public transport? The bus stops close by.

What language are the classes in?

Classes are bilingual: Dutch and English. Everyone joins in.

See you Monday? 🌿

Come as you are. No experience or sports outfit needed — just 45 minutes for yourself (or together with your little one). Join the seven classes from 19 October.
