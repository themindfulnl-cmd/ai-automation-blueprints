Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

1. Who We Are

The Mindful Kids is a children's yoga and mindfulness program based in the Netherlands, operated by Gagan Deep. We are committed to protecting the privacy of our participants, especially children and their families. Contact: themindfulnl@gmail.com.

2. What Data We Collect

We collect the following personal data only when you voluntarily provide it:

  • Name and email address (via our contact form, ebook downloads, or trial class registration)
  • Your child's age range (when booking classes)
  • Location preference (Amstelveen, Hoofddorp, or Nieuw-Vennep)
  • Any health information you choose to share for class safety purposes

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data only for the following purposes:

  • To deliver ebooks and free resources you request
  • To communicate about classes, schedules, and programs
  • To send you relevant updates (only if you opt in)

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is processed through Resend (email service) and stored securely. We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties. We retain your data only as long as necessary for the purposes described above.

5. Children's Privacy

We take children's privacy very seriously. We do not collect personal data directly from children. All data is provided by parents or guardians. We never share children's information with third parties. Photos or videos from classes are only used with explicit written parental consent.

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to: access your personal data, correct inaccurate data, request deletion of your data, withdraw consent at any time, and file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens). To exercise any of these rights, contact us at themindfulnl@gmail.com.

7. Cookies

Our website uses only essential cookies required for the website to function. We do not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy and request your consent.

Questions about your privacy?: themindfulnl@gmail.com
