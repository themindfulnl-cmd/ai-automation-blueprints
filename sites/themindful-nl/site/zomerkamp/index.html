the same heart, a new adventure each time

One Heart Detective camp. A fresh theme for every holiday.

The heart of the camp stays the same: recognising feelings, practising calm and building confidence through yoga, breathing and creativity. The stories, detective missions and creative activities change with each holiday, giving returning detectives something new to discover.

the mission

Three days. Three cases to crack.

Every morning opens a new case: collect clues, interview witnesses, go on stakeout and treasure hunts — and unlock a real superpower by the end. Yoga, breath and craft are the detective's secret tools.

DAY 1
01
Case #001 · Resilience

The Case of the Vanished Courage

A real Dutch astronaut — André Kuipers — is frozen at the space‑station hatch. The Doubt Shadow stole his courage and hid it in 5 secret letters. Your mission: find them all and bring his bravery back.

  • Take the Detective Oath + earn your badge
  • Blast off through space: rocket, star & landing poses
  • Meet the 3 friends: Grumpy, Captain Calm & Hooray
  • Crack the 5 secret clues to free Astronaut André
  • Field break + field notes in nature
  • Forensic lab: build your Strength Stone
  • Case closed: debrief + celebration jump

Frequently asked questions

Is Heart Detective the same camp every school holiday?

No. The core remains yoga, breathing, creativity and learning to recognise feelings, but the stories, missions and activities change each holiday. Themes can explore autumn and health, Christmas and winter, or spring and new discoveries.

What is a Heart Detective holiday camp?

A small, bilingual camp for children aged 5–12. Children move, breathe, create, play and solve imaginative missions while practising practical skills for confidence and emotional regulation.

Where and when is the next holiday camp?

The Heart Detective autumn camp is on Monday 12 October 2026 from 13:00 to 16:00 at Faraja Yoga in Hoofddorp. It is one afternoon, not a multi-day camp. It runs with at least eight confirmed registrations.

What age is the camp for?

For children aged 5 to 12. We work in small groups and adapt activities to age, energy and what each child needs.

Is the camp in Dutch or English?

Both. Guidance is bilingual in Dutch and English, so Dutch and international children can join together.

How much does the next camp cost?

The 12 October afternoon costs €38 per child. You can register without paying now; we will let you know when the camp is confirmed to run.

Does my child need yoga experience?

No. Everything is playful and beginner-friendly. Comfortable clothes, water and a snack are usually enough; you receive the final packing list before camp day.

Is this suitable for a shy or easily overwhelmed child?

The small group, calm pacing and clear structure often suit sensitive children. Tell us what helps your child and we will honestly discuss whether the upcoming edition is a good match.
