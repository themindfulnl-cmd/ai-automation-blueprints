5 Breathing Games That Really Calm Children

Proven exercises you can try with your child tonight. Used by 200+ Dutch families. Instant delivery to your inbox.

What's inside this free guide?

🌬️5 step-by-step breathing exercises with clear instructions
📖Illustrated guide — easy to follow for ages 5-12
👶Works for all children — calm, energetic, sensitive, or gifted
📥Instant delivery as PDF to your email inbox

The 5 exercises you'll learn:

1

Belly Balloon Breath

Place hands on belly, breathe in (balloon fills!), breathe out (deflates). Calms the nervous system in 30 seconds.

2

Heart Hug Breath

Cross arms on chest, breathe in love, breathe out worry. Perfect for bedtime anxiety.

3

Ocean Breath

Breathe like waves — slow in, long out. Creates deep calm after school meltdowns.

4

Tiger's Determined Breath

Strong inhale through the nose, powerful exhale. Builds confidence before tests or new situations.

5

Rainbow Breath

Breathe in all 7 colours one by one. A complete reset for overwhelm and big emotions.
