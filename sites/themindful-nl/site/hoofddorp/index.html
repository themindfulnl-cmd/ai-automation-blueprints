FAQ

You can find The Mindful Kids on Thursdays at Faraja Yoga, Nijverheidsstraat 3A+B (2132 AZ). There are groups for ages 5–9 and 10–12. Check the schedule for current places.

The first trial class is free. Classes then cost €15 each. You can register without payment through the live class list.

Our weekly kids yoga classes in Hoofddorp are designed for children aged 5–12. We work in small groups so every child feels seen and heard.

No, not at all. The Rainbow Feelings method is built for beginners — we use play, stories, breathing exercises, and imagination. Every child joins at their own level.

Each class is 45 minutes and follows a predictable flow: welcome circle, balance games, sun salutations, the 3 Friends + Story Yoga, and a closing rain dance with body scan.

Yes. We visit primary schools in Hoofddorp and across Noord-Holland for mindfulness, breathwork, and emotional-intelligence workshops for ages 5–12. Reach out at themindfulnl@gmail.com for a school visit.

Rainbow Feelings is our emotional-literacy framework. Kids learn to recognise and name big feelings through three signature friends: General Grumpy (big uncomfortable feelings), Captain Calm (the breath), and Hip Hip Hooray (the celebration).
