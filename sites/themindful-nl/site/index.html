A Clear Picture: The Growing Need for Emotional Well-being

Why This Matters: The Growing Need for Emotional Well-being

Figures vary by year and age group, but the message is clear: children and young people need accessible, practical support early.

20% → 32%

Rising emotional waves

Among Dutch 16–20-year-olds, moderate anxiety and depression symptoms rose from 20.2% in 2016 to 31.9% in 2020.

52%

The quiet ache of loneliness

In March 2022, 52% of young people felt lonely. By December 2024 this was 43% — lower, but still substantial.

24%

Girls bearing the brunt

In 2021, 24% of Dutch girls and young women aged 12–25 reported poor mental health, compared with 18% of all young people.

Sources: Dutch adolescent study, RIVM & CBS.

The Program: The Critical Advantage

Building Confident Kids, Early

These workshops give children aged 5–12 a playful place to practise naming feelings, breathing, movement, and reflection.

Why Start Now?

Children can learn simple routines now and practise using them at school, at home, and during transitions.

Lasting Impact

Repeated practice helps children remember the vocabulary and exercises when they want to pause or reset.

What Your Child Gains

  • Early Emotional Literacy

    Kids learn to understand and manage feelings, building vital emotional literacy early.

  • A Foundation for Lifelong Well-Being

    We establish early habits for self-awareness, self-care, balance, and mental health, setting them on a path to sustained well-being.

  • Practical Coping Tools

    Gain hands-on tools (breathing, grounding) for stress, frustration, and anxiety.

  • Boosted Confidence & Resilience

    They develop a stronger sense of self and self-belief, learning to navigate setbacks and bounce back with inner strength.

  • Stronger Friendships & Social Skills

    Improved empathy, communication, and conflict resolution lead to healthier, more meaningful peer interactions.

Before you book

Discuss your needs →
Which organisations and ages are the programmes for?+

We offer programmes for primary schools (groups 3–8), BSO and childcare. First Steps is designed specifically for childcare groups aged 4–6.

How long does a session take?+

A school or Superfriends session usually takes 45 minutes. Theme days and holiday programmes receive a tailored schedule, such as a two-hour Heart Detective programme.

Do you deliver at our location?+

Yes. We deliver programmes at schools, BSO and childcare locations across Noord-Holland. We agree the space, group format and practical schedule beforehand.

Are the programmes available in Dutch or English?+

Both are available. We agree the language in advance based on your children, team and location.

How do we receive a proposal and price?+

Tell us the age group, group size, location and preferred format. We will then prepare a tailored proposal for a workshop, theme day or programme series.

For parents

Rainbow Feelings is designed for children aged 5-9. The exercises are adapted per age group, so both younger and older children get the most out of each session.

For the first session, parents are welcome to stay. After that, children usually feel comfortable on their own. You'll receive a recap after each class with exercises to practice at home.

Rainbow Feelings combines yoga, breathing, emotional intelligence, and storytelling into one method. Each session follows the 7-colour system where children learn to identify, express, and manage their emotions through movement and play.

The Mindful Kids is based in Hoofddorp. The Classes page lists confirmed weekly groups. School, BSO, childcare, camp, and private programmes can also be arranged in Amstelveen, Amsterdam, Haarlem, Nieuw-Vennep, and surrounding areas.

A standard Rainbow Feelings programme runs for 7 weeks (one colour per week), with a celebration session in week 8. Each session is 45-60 minutes depending on the age group.
