FAQ

There is not yet a fixed weekly class in Amsterdam. You can join the interest list for free; once eight families are interested, we will find a suitable venue and schedule the series.

Our classes in Amsterdam are designed for children aged 5 to 12. We adapt the exercises to each child's level and developmental stage.

The price will be announced once the venue and start date are set. Joining the interest list is free and does not commit you to anything.

Open the live class list, choose Amsterdam and enter your details. Your registration counts after you open the link in the confirmation email.

No. The Rainbow Feelings method is built for beginners. We use play, stories, breathing games, and imagination — every child joins at their own level.

Each class is 45 minutes and follows a predictable flow: welcome circle, balance games, sun salutations, the 3 Friends + Story Yoga, and a closing rain dance with body scan.

Rainbow Feelings is our emotional-literacy framework. Kids learn to recognise big feelings through three signature friends: General Grumpy (big feelings), Captain Calm (the breath), and Hip Hip Hooray (the celebration).
